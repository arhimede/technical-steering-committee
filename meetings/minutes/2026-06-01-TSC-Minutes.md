## Agenda

### Use the Laminas budget to pay for new design 

Julian writes: We must agree that we need a new professional and modern design for:

- getlaminas.org website
- documentation website

I am proposing to use the designer that made the Mezzio logo to handle both of those tasks, and use Laminas current budget for this.

#### Discussion

There was immediate push-back, questioning what the motivation is for a branding/design change.
The feedback was along the lines of "what is the expected outcome of this, and why is it important?"
Matthew suggested having measurable goals (e.g. increase downloads, attract new users, etc.).
Julian said that this would be the first part of a larger effort, which would include refactoring the documentation site and showing donors we're doing something useful with their donations.
Matthew and Luís pushed back, asking how we would position this to donors as a useful effort (with some suggestions as well).

Julian said the point is to prove that Laminas and Mezzio are not dead; Luís asked what we're really trying to prove here.
Eventually, we boiled it down to something along the lines of "how do we make business leaders and professionals care and choose Laminas and Mezzio?"
With that, the idea of a design change becomes something further along the line; Matthew suggested we should have the following path:

1. Get alignment on where we fit in the PHP ecosystem (e.g. maintainable, minimalist framework you can extend and rely on)
1. Determine what messaging will communicate that.
1. Determine if there are other actions we need to take (tutorials, videos, documentation, etc.) that will move us in that direction
1. Create a marketing plan around that (which may be website redesign and/or branding change, but could be social media strategy, newsletters, etc.)

In other words, determine where we fit in the ecosystem, identify what messaging and actions will resonate, and _then_ think about branding and design.

#### Decisions

No decisions today, as no quorum.
However, we agreed to continue discussions asynchronously, with a goal of having some form of actionable vote in the July meeting.
